📎 Workspace Evidence Binder ?

Combine saved evidence across the workspace into one consolidated list, then sort or cluster it for easier review.

Tip: The Binder consolidates evidence links from your workspace and de-duplicates by URL. Use the Sort and View controls to organize results (by date, type, or condition). Toggle the viewer to open a readable list; clustered view groups related items. Label-only entries (no URL) are included — use the "Show label-only nodes" graph toggle to locate them visually.

🕸️ Evidence Relationship Graph ?

Visualize how records, notes, and statements connect so you can spot isolated evidence and stronger clusters quickly.

Tip: The Evidence Relationship Graph shows how evidence items relate to each other. Nodes represent unique evidence items (grouped by URL or label-only entries). Use "Hide orphans" to focus on connected clusters. Drag nodes to reposition and scroll to zoom. Toggle "Show label-only nodes" to include or hide items that have only labels (no URL).

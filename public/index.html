      Privacy & Safety Notice

      Educational use only. VA CFR Finder does not provide legal advice, medical advice, representation, or a guarantee of claim outcomes.

      • Local data: claim packets, notes, timelines, evidence links, and task state may be saved in this browser.
      • Signed-in sync: account users sync claim packet data to this app server. Do not paste highly sensitive medical details unless you understand that risk.
      • No document upload review: evidence links are references you provide; the app does not verify private records.
      • Verify sources: always check current eCFR/VA guidance and consider an accredited representative.

      Report incorrect CFR info or share feedback

      Please do not include SSNs, claim numbers, full medical records, or other private identifiers.

      Open email

      Feedback is stored without claim identifiers unless you type them. Please keep it general.
